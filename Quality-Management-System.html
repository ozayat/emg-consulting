What makes EMG One of the Leading Providers in the Environmental Expertise 

The goal is to ensure the long-term viability of societal development and the use of water, land, and air resources. 

This is accomplished by managing these resources to minimize environmental pollution and degradation. To do this, they must:

1. Plan and design water systems.    2. Develop wastewater treatment facilities.    3. Analyze surface and groundwater quality. 

4. Design soil remediation systems.  5. Create plans for waste collection and disposal.  6. Reuse wastewater and sludge
