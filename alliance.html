International Water Technology Association

Founded by experts and professors as non-governmental association no. 1623 of 2004, and restructured in October 2010 into an international association.

IWTA aims

What the association sets out to do.

  • Highlight the importance of the water issue socially and economically at local, national and international level
  • Highlight water and resource preservation
  • Spread modern water treatment and desalination technology
  • Organise training courses
  • Organise scientific and awareness meetings
  • Conduct research on aquatic resources and water management

Institutions & collaboration
