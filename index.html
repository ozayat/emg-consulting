Core practice

Three environments, one engineering discipline.

Pollution does not respect boundaries between media. We work across water, air and land with the same analytical rigour — measurement, modelling, design, and verification.

Water

Water Pollution Control

Control of water pollution requires appropriate infrastructure and management plans as well as legislation — from treatment process design through to compliance monitoring.

Air

Air Pollution Control

Air quality is closely related to the earth's climate and ecosystems globally. We assess dispersion, model microclimate and heat waste, and design abatement strategies.

Land

Environmental Remediation

Removal of pollution and contaminants from environmental media — soil, groundwater, sediment and surface water — supported by site characterisation and laboratory analysis.
